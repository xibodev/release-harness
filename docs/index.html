DETERMINISTIC RELEASE ADJUDICATION

A release cannot be talked into green.

Release-Harness runs project-owned quality gates and local UAT. Define your contracts, exercise your product, and inspect a deterministic verdict from sealed evidence.

THE TRUST MODEL

Intelligence can investigate. Only evidence can decide.

The Node.js core uses Docker, Playwright, and project-owned probes to collect evidence. Optional AI playbooks help prepare contracts and investigate failures; they do not decide the gate.

  1. 01Project contractsTopology, origins, scenarios
  2. 02MaterializerDetached source workspace
  3. 03Compose & PlaywrightOrigins + Egress Filter
  4. 04Sealer & verdictSHA-256 Manifest

Evidence hashes detect changes relative to the seal, not a compromised execution host. Browser filtering is not container-wide isolation. Read the architecture and trust boundaries.

VERDICT REGISTER

Five exit codes. No ambiguous green.

The evaluator derives the outcome from recorded evidence and declared policy. A pass covers that gate, not checks you never declared.

0
PASS

The declared gate passed with verified evidence.

Not a deployment or a production guarantee.
1
FAIL

Observed failure or early rejection without a verdict.

Inspect causes; fix product defects or acquire missing fixtures.
2
UNPROVEN

Unmet conditions, waivers, or dirty development.

Resolve underlying failures before clean certification.
3
HARNESS_ERROR

Contract, environment, probe or startup fault.

Inspect startup evidence. UNKNOWN is unresolved attribution, not a product bug.
4
EVIDENCE_INVALID

Evidence tampering or checksum mismatch.

Preserve the run and investigate; no blind cleanup or resealing.
Read exit-code reference

Bring the harness into your project.

Start with contracts and a smoke scenario for your real application. Use the CLI yourself or add AI assistance later. No AI host is required.

The published release is 2.0.0 and requires Node.js 20+. Node.js 20 is the CI-tested baseline. Upgrading from 1.2.0? Read versions & upgrades.
